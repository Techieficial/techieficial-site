## Two ways to never miss a call

If you are missing calls, the two most common fixes are a traditional answering service, where people answer on your behalf, and an AI receptionist, where an AI voice agent answers. Both can work. The right choice depends on what you need the call to achieve.

## What each one does

**An answering service** takes messages and, depending on the plan, can follow simple scripts. A person answers, which some callers prefer. Most services send you the message and leave the follow-up to you.

**An AI receptionist** answers every call, day and night, using the information and rules you give it. It can answer common questions, capture details, and book appointments directly into your calendar. It can also send details straight into your CRM and trigger a follow-up text.

## How they compare

| | Answering service | AI receptionist |
|---|---|---|
| Availability | Depends on plan and staffing | Every hour of every day |
| Booking appointments | Sometimes, often by message | Directly into your calendar |
| Connection to your CRM | Usually a message or email | Details logged and tagged automatically |
| Handling unusual calls | Strong, a person can improvise | Hands over to your team by rule |
| Cost structure | Often per minute or per call | Usually a monthly fee plus usage |

## When an answering service makes sense

- Your callers expect to speak to a person every time.
- Calls are unusual and hard to predict.
- You only need messages taken, not bookings made.

## When an AI receptionist makes sense

- Many calls ask the same questions, such as prices, hours and availability.
- You want calls to turn straight into bookings.
- You want every caller logged in your CRM with a follow-up.

## A mixed approach

Many businesses use both. The AI receptionist handles routine calls and bookings, and hands anything complex or urgent to a person. This keeps your team focused on the calls that need them.
