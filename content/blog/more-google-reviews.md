## Most happy customers never leave a review

Not because they don't want to. Nobody asked them at the right moment, or the link was hard to find. Reviews are one of the first things people check before they call you, so it pays to ask every time.

## Ask at the right moment

The best time to ask is right after the job is done, while the customer is still pleased. Not a week later, and not in the middle of the invoice.

## Make it one tap

Send a short text with a direct link to your Google review page. No logging in, no searching for your business. The easier it is, the more people do it.

## Check how it went first

Start with a simple question: "How did we do today?" Customers who had a great experience get the review link. Customers who had a problem are sent to you privately, so you can fix it before it becomes a public one-star review.

## Remind once, then stop

One friendly reminder is fine. More than that starts to feel pushy.

## Reply to every review

Thank people for good reviews and respond calmly to bad ones. It shows future customers that you care, and it helps your profile.

## Make it automatic

Doing this by hand after every job is hard to keep up. The [AI Reviews Agent](/ai-team/reviews-agent) sends the request after every job, routes unhappy feedback to you and drafts replies for you to approve.
