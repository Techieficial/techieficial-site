## The call nobody wants to miss

It's 9:15 PM. A homeowner has water coming through the ceiling. They will call the first company that picks up. If your phone goes to voicemail, they hang up and try the next one.

Here is what happens when your AI Receptionist answers instead.

## 1. It answers on the first ring

The AI Receptionist picks up straight away, greets the caller with your business name and asks what's going on. There is no hold music and no menu to press through.

## 2. It listens for your emergency words

You decide what counts as an emergency. For a plumber that might be "burst pipe", "flooding" or "no water". For HVAC it might be "no heat" or "gas smell". When the caller uses one of those words, the AI treats the call as urgent.

## 3. It transfers the call to your on-call number

Urgent calls are transferred straight to you or whoever is on call, following the rules you set. If nobody picks up, it takes the caller's details and tells them when to expect a call back, so they don't keep dialing other companies.

## 4. It sends you a summary

You get a short summary by text: name, address, the problem and what happened on the call. The contact is logged and tagged in your CRM, so nothing is lost if you're busy.

## 5. Routine calls still get booked

Not every call is an emergency. For routine jobs, the AI Receptionist checks your calendar and books a time, then sends the caller a confirmation.

## What it never does

It never promises a time you can't keep and never quotes a price you haven't approved. If it isn't sure, it hands the call to a person.
