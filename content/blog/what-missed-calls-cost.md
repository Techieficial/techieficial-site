## Most businesses miss more calls than they realise

When you are busy with a customer, on a job or closing up for the day, the phone keeps ringing. A study by 411 Locals tracked calls to 85 small businesses across 58 industries and found that [62% of calls went unanswered](https://411locals.us/small-business-owners-dont-answer-62-of-phone-calls/). Calls either went to voicemail or were not picked up at all.

The study is from 2016 and covers a small sample, so treat the exact number with care. The pattern is still familiar to most owners: the calls you miss cluster around the times you are busiest.

## Why a missed call usually means a lost customer

A person who calls a service business often has a problem right now. A leaking pipe, a broken tooth or a legal deadline does not wait for a callback tomorrow. If nobody answers, the easiest next step is to call the next business on the list.

Speed matters for web leads too. Research published in Harvard Business Review found that firms that contacted a new online lead within an hour were [nearly seven times as likely to qualify that lead](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads) as firms that waited even one more hour.

## How to estimate what missed calls cost you

You do not need industry averages. Your own numbers are more useful:

1. **Count missed calls for two weeks.** Your phone system or carrier can usually show missed and unanswered calls.
2. **Estimate how many were new customers.** Look at the numbers you do not recognise.
3. **Use your normal close rate.** If you win 3 out of 10 new callers you speak to, apply the same rate.
4. **Multiply by your average job value.**

For example, 20 missed new-customer calls a month, a 30% close rate and a $500 average job adds up to about $3,000 a month in work that went elsewhere. Your numbers will differ, but the calculation shows whether this problem is worth fixing.

## Three fixes, from simplest to most complete

- **Missed-call text back.** When a call is missed, the caller gets a text within moments. Many people reply by text, which keeps the conversation going.
- **Speed-to-lead replies.** Every form and ad lead gets an instant reply and your team gets a task to follow up.
- **An AI receptionist.** An AI voice agent answers the call, handles common questions, books appointments and passes urgent calls to you.

All three work best when they feed into one CRM, so you can see which calls turned into booked work.
