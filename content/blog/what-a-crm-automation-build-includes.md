## Why a build, not just a login

Buying CRM software gives you an empty system. The value comes from the build: the pipelines, automations, templates and connections that match how your business actually sells. Here is what a complete build includes.

## 1. Pipeline and contacts

We start by mapping how a lead becomes a customer in your business. That becomes your pipeline, with clear stages such as new lead, contacted, booked and won. Existing contacts are imported and tagged so they can be used in campaigns.

## 2. Lead capture

Every way a lead can reach you is connected: website forms, ad forms, phone calls, text messages and chat. Each lead lands in the CRM with its source recorded, so you know which marketing produced it.

## 3. Speed-to-lead

Speed matters. Research published in Harvard Business Review found that firms contacting new online leads within an hour were [nearly seven times as likely to qualify them](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads). So every new lead gets an instant reply by text and email, and your team gets a task to call.

## 4. Missed-call text back

When a call is missed, the caller gets a text straight away. This simple step keeps many conversations alive that would otherwise be lost.

## 5. Nurture and reactivation

Not every lead is ready to buy today. Nurture sequences send useful follow-ups over days and weeks. Reactivation campaigns bring back old leads and past customers.

## 6. Booking and reminders

Leads can book a time online. Confirmations and reminders go out automatically to reduce no-shows.

## 7. Reviews

After a job or appointment, happy customers get a request to leave a review, at the moment they are most likely to say yes.

## 8. Reporting

A simple dashboard shows where leads come from, how fast they are contacted and how many turn into booked work.

## 9. Testing, training and handover

Before launch, every path is tested end to end. Your team gets training and a recorded walkthrough, and we keep monitoring and improving the system after go-live.
