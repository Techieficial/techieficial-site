## The lead is shopping around

When someone fills in a quote form on your website, they have usually filled in a few others too. The business that replies first gets the first real conversation, and often the job.

A [Harvard Business Review study](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads) found that companies contacting leads within an hour were far more likely to qualify them than those that waited longer. Every minute you wait, the lead cools.

## Why most businesses reply slowly

It isn't laziness. You're on a job, in a meeting or asleep. Leads come in from your website, Facebook, Instagram and ad forms, and they land in different places. By the time you see them, hours have passed.

## How to reply in under a minute

1. **Send every lead to one place.** Connect your website forms, ad lead forms and social messages to your CRM.
2. **Reply automatically, but like a person.** A short, friendly text that uses their name and asks one useful question works better than a generic "Thanks for your enquiry".
3. **Ask the questions you would ask.** What service, where, and when. Then offer a time to book.
4. **Keep following up.** If they don't reply, try again over the next few days, and stop the moment they answer or opt out.
