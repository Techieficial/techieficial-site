Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. This policy explains which ones we use.

## Necessary cookies

These make the site work and remember your cookie choices. They cannot be switched off.

- **Consent choice:** remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies.
- **Campaign source:** remembers which ad or link brought you to the site, so an enquiry can be credited to the right campaign. It is stored only on your device and sent to us only if you submit a form.
- **Spam protection:** our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated spam.

## Analytics cookies (optional)

With your permission we use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and where people get stuck.

## Marketing cookies (optional)

With your permission we use the Meta Pixel to measure the results of our ads and show relevant ads to people who visited the site.

## Your choices

When you first visit, you can accept or reject optional cookies, or choose by category. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
