We want everyone to be able to use techieficial.com, including people who use assistive technology.

## Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Test every page with automated accessibility checks before it goes live.
- Make every menu, form and interactive element usable with a keyboard.
- Keep text contrast at or above WCAG AA levels.
- Respect your device's "reduce motion" setting by switching off non-essential animation.
- Provide text alternatives for images and captions or transcripts for video where possible.

## Known limits

Some content from third parties, such as embedded calendars or videos, may not fully meet these standards. We choose providers with accessibility in mind and look for alternatives when problems are reported.

## Report a problem

If you have trouble using any part of the site, please tell us through our [contact page](/contact). Include the page and a short description of the problem, and we will work to fix it.
