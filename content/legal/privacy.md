This policy explains how Techieficial ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit techieficial.com or contact us.

## Information we collect

- **Details you give us:** your name, email address, phone number, business details and anything you write in a form.
- **Booking details:** the date and time of any call you book with us.
- **Usage information:** if you accept analytics cookies, we collect information about how you use the site, such as pages visited and the device and browser you use.
- **Campaign information:** where you arrived from, such as an ad or email, so we know which marketing works.

## How we use it

- To reply to your enquiry and provide the services you ask for.
- To schedule and hold calls and demos.
- To send you messages you have agreed to receive, such as our newsletter or text messages.
- To understand and improve how our website and marketing perform.
- To meet legal and security obligations.

## How we share it

We use trusted service providers to run our business, such as our CRM, calendar, hosting, email and analytics providers. They only process your information on our behalf. We do not sell your personal information.

## Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages, see our [SMS terms](/sms-terms). Your mobile number and consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

## Cookies

We use necessary cookies to run the site, and analytics and marketing cookies only if you accept them. See our [cookie policy](/cookies).

## How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law, then delete or anonymise it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, [contact us](/contact).

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our [contact page](/contact).
