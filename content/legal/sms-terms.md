## Program description

By checking the SMS consent box on a form on techieficial.com and submitting your mobile number, you agree to receive text messages from Techieficial about your enquiry, bookings, demos and our services.

## Opting in

Consent is optional. It is never required to buy any product or service. The consent box on our forms is unchecked by default and you choose whether to tick it.

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your enquiry and bookings.

## Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

## How to stop

Reply **STOP** to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that.

## Help

Reply **HELP** to any message for help, or reach us through our [contact page](/contact).

## Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy

Your mobile number and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. See our [privacy policy](/privacy) for how we handle personal information.
