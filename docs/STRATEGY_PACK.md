## 1. Business requirements (BRD)

The website has one job: turn service business owners into booked strategy calls for the AI Front Office, with demo tests and low-ticket sales as the paths for everyone not ready yet.

**Business goals the site serves:**

1. Fill 3 to 4 AI Front Office builds a month (the delivery cap for one person)
2. Sell AI Receptionist Starter to owners who mainly lose phone calls
3. Win 3 founding clients at a discount in exchange for a testimonial and case study
4. Build an audience (newsletter, Academy waitlist) for later offers
5. Prove Techieficial runs on its own AI team ("We run on it")

**Offers the site sells (draft prices from the CRM):**

Usage (phone, SMS, email, AI minutes) is billed at cost on every monthly plan.

**What this means for the site:**

- **Category name conflict:** Avoca already uses "The AI Front Office" on its HVAC pages. Keep "AI front office" as a description, but lead with our own name for the offer, and run a trademark check before printing it on anything. (Owner decision pending.)
- **Price anchoring:** show the cost of a part-time receptionist and a human answering service next to our prices, not the $49 apps.
- **Our edge to prove on every page:** one connected system (answer, follow up, remind, review, reactivate), trained for the trade, checked every week by a person, reported in booked jobs.
- **Win against DIY apps:** "You don't have time to set it up and babysit it. We do."
- **Win against Avoca-class platforms:** no ServiceTitan required, live in 10 to 14 days, priced for 5 to 30 person teams.

## 4. Customer journeys

Every journey has the same spine: see the problem, hear the AI work, then talk to a person. Personas differ only in where they enter and what proof they need.

**The core journey (AI Front Office, $3,500 + $1,297/mo):**

1. **Discover:** Meta ad, Google search or a social post lands on an industry page or the homepage
2. **Recognise the problem:** sourced stat on missed calls, missed-call calculator
3. **Experience it:** calls the AI Receptionist from the Demo Lab, or requests a "call me"
4. **See what happened:** the Demo Lab shows the CRM steps (contact logged, text sent, task created)
5. **Commit lightly:** books a free Missed Lead Audit or the assessment
6. **Talk:** 30-minute strategy call; qualification score from the Offers Pack decides the route
7. **Buy:** proposal, then payment through the CRM
8. **Onboard:** intake form, kickoff call, live in 10 to 14 days

Leads that stall at any step drop into the follow-up agent's nurture sequence; nobody is left without a next step.

**Entry points and differences by persona:**

| Persona | Most likely entry | Key proof on the way | Path if not ready |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mike, home services | Meta ad to the home services page | Live call, after-hours example, price table | AI Receptionist Starter or Missed-Call Text-Back ($297) |
| Sarah, tradie | Google search "missed calls tradie" or a blog post | Quote follow-up flow, Starter pricing | AI Receptionist Playbook ($97) |
| Priya, rental agency | LinkedIn post or search | Landlord and tenant workflow story, data ownership | Missed Lead Audit, newsletter |
| Tony, gym owner | Instagram or TikTok video | Trial booking demo, no-show rebook | Missed-Call Text-Back, newsletter |
| Dana, agency owner | LinkedIn or referral to the Agencies page | NDA promise, snapshot quality, turnaround | Partner call later; newsletter |

**Low-ticket journey (under $3,000):** product page, then direct checkout through the CRM payment link, then an upsell email to the strategy call 7 days later.

**Founding-client journey:** founding offer page (3 places, discounted build), short application form, strategy call, then signed agreement that includes the testimonial and case study terms.
