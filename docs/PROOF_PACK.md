## 1. Proof rules

The site shows real work instead of reviews: 7 project stories from builds the founder did himself, a "We run on it" page, live demos, and up to 3 founding clients who earn a real testimonial. Every claim below comes from facts the owner stated; nothing is invented.

| We show | We never show |
| --- | --- |
| Real Loom walkthroughs of builds the founder did | Made-up quotes, star ratings or review schema |
| Facts the owner stated (workflow counts, build time, members, calls per day) | Results numbers that were never measured ("300% more bookings") |
| Client names only where the owner confirms it's allowed | Client data, phone numbers or contacts visible in videos |
| "Founder's past work" label on stories built before Techieficial | Past clients presented as Techieficial clients |
| Founding-client testimonials labelled as discounted | Paid or discounted reviews without saying so |

**Why the last row matters:** under US FTC endorsement rules, a testimonial from a client who got a discount must say so. Each founding-client testimonial will carry a short note such as "Founding client: received a discounted build."

**Before any story goes live:** check each Loom for visible client data (names, phones, emails) and blur or trim it, and confirm there is no NDA that blocks naming the client.

| Minimum term | 3 months | 3 months |
